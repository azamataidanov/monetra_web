Privacy Policy

1. Summary

Monetra is a personal finance tracker. By default, your accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, debts, savings goals, and settings are stored locally on your device, not in our own cloud database. We do not require you to create an account to use the App.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, it may store on your device:

  • Account / balance names, currencies, and starting balances
  • Income and expense transactions (amounts, categories, dates, notes)
  • Budgets, category budgets, recurring payments, debts, and savings goals
  • Exchange rates you enter manually
  • App settings (theme, currency, notification and biometric preferences)
  • Local backup files you create or import
  • Premium / subscription entitlement status on this device

3. Optional AI insights (Premium)

If you use Premium AI insights, the App may send a finance snapshot (summary of spending, income, budgets, debts, recurring items, and goals — not your Google account password) through our AI proxy to a third-party AI provider (for example Google Gemini) to generate advice.

  • We use this transmission to generate a response.
  • We do not operate a permanent cloud archive of your full transaction history.
  • We do not sell your financial data.
  • The third-party provider processes the request under its own terms; only use AI if you accept this Policy and those terms.

4. Subscriptions and payments

If you purchase Monetra Premium, payment and subscription management are handled by Google Play on Android. We receive purchase / entitlement status to unlock Premium features. We do not receive your full payment card details.

5. Permissions and device features

  • Internet — AI insights, loading where applicable, and sharing
  • Microphone — only when you choose Add by voice; used for on-device / system speech recognition while that feature is open
  • Notifications — optional budget / recurring / debt reminders
  • Biometric / device lock — optional app lock
  • Storage / file access — when you export, backup, or restore files you choose

6. Export, backup, and sharing

If you export CSV/PDF, share a backup, or restore data, you choose what leaves your device. We are not responsible for how you or others store or share those files.

7. What we do not do by default

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for advertising profiles in the default App configuration.
  • We do not require a Monetra login account for core tracking.

8. Analytics and crash data

Google may provide aggregated analytics or crash diagnostics if you opt in through your device or store tools. If we add our own analytics later, this Policy will be updated.

9. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your Monetra data without contacting us:

  1. Open Monetra on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Clear all data (or the equivalent delete / reset action) and confirm.
  4. Optionally uninstall the App to remove remaining local App data from that device.

Alternative request: email aza.aidanov@gmail.com with subject “Monetra data deletion” and your Google Play order ID (if you purchased Premium).

10. Children

The App is intended for users 16+ (or older where required by local law). It is not directed to children under 13.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
